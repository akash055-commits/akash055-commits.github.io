03 — Views

How I think about growth.

On measurement vs. attribution

Attribution tells you which pipe the water came out of. Incrementality tells you if you needed the pipe. Once you've run a geo-holdout across 12 cities and watched a channel vanish without the topline moving, you stop arguing about last-click.

On AI in performance marketing

Most “AI in marketing” posts are prompt libraries. The real wins are boring: scoring creative before it ships, generating 15K variants when you'd previously brief 15. Hype is on image generation; leverage is on the filter that runs after.

On scope vs. depth

The trap for technical growth folks is becoming the one pulled into every cross-team thing — CAPI wiring, dashboard fixes, Zapier flows — instead of compounding in one channel. I've done the cross-team work; I'm deliberate now about owning a channel's P&L.
